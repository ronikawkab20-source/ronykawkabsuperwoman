Why training, and not another diet

From your thirties, the body quietly gives muscle away.

Less muscle means a slower metabolism, weaker bones, a back that complains, and less energy for the same day you always did. Dieting alone speeds that up — you lose weight and the muscle underneath it.

Training puts it back. That is the honest version of anti-ageing: not looking younger in a photo, but moving like you did ten years ago — carrying your kids, climbing your stairs, standing straight, sleeping properly.

Before you ask

Fair questions.

I’ve never trained before. Is this too much for me?

No. Every exercise is on video, and Rony sets your starting level on the call. Beginners are most of who he works with.

Do I need a gym?

No. The same program comes in a gym version, a home version with little or no equipment, and short sessions you can do at work. You choose on the call, and you can switch during the 30 days.

What does “anti-ageing” actually mean here?

Strength, posture, energy and keeping the muscle that the years take away. It is training and food, not a treatment, and nobody is promising you a younger face.

How much weight will I lose?

Nobody honest can tell you that in advance, and Rony won’t. Results depend on you, your body and your health.

How do I pay?

After your call with Rony, he sends you a Whish payment link on WhatsApp. One payment of $369. Nothing is taken before you’ve spoken to him.

How is this different from the 90-day program?

The 90-day program is daily one-to-one coaching, a fully custom plan and check-in calls with Rony. This is the training app, the nutrition plan, one call to set you up, and WhatsApp when you need him. Less hand-holding, lower price.

I have a health condition or an old injury.

Tell Rony on the call. He trains around injuries every day. For some conditions he’ll ask you to check with your doctor first, and for some he’ll tell you this isn’t the right thing for you.
